Cart

The cart that slides in when someone adds a product, and the full cart page. Here you set up the free delivery bar, spend rewards, free gifts and add-ons. Most of it is in the Theme editor (Online Store › Themes › Customize) › Theme settings.

What's in the cart

  • Progress bar: shows how far the shopper is from free delivery, and up to two spend rewards, for example £10 off £50.
  • Free gift chooser: the shopper picks one free product once their cart has a qualifying product.
  • Gift with purchase: a gift the shopper can add once they spend a set amount.
  • Add-ons: another product offered with an Add on + button.
  • Savings: discounted items show the old price struck through. Free gifts show what they're worth and Free.
The cart only shows the offer Spend rewards and free gifts also need a discount in Shopify admin › Discounts. Without it, the cart shows the reward but the shopper still pays full price at checkout.

Set up the free delivery bar

  1. Go to Theme editor › Theme settings › Carts and turn on Enable under Free Shipping Progress bar.
  2. Set Shipping Theshold in pence or cents, for example 4000 for £40.
  3. Write the message in xx away from FREE UK delivery. Put [amount] where the amount left should go, for example "[amount] away from FREE UK delivery".
  4. Write the Success message, shown once the shopper reaches free delivery.
  5. To show free delivery as a banner at the top of the cart instead of on the bar, tick Make shipping a notice.
  6. Click Save.

To give subscribers free delivery whatever they spend, tick Free Shipping on default market Subs?, and the DE and ES options for those markets.

Add spend rewards to the progress bar

Up to two rewards after free delivery, for example £10 off £50 and £25 off £100. Only products from one collection count towards them.

  1. In Shopify admin › Discounts, create an automatic discount for each reward, for the same collection and minimum spend. Set its start and end dates.
  2. Go to Theme editor › Theme settings › Carts › Spend discount checkpoints.
  3. Choose the Qualifying collection: the same collection as the discount.
  4. Tick Enable checkpoint 2 and set its threshold in pence, for example 5000 for £50.
  5. Write the Checkpoint 2 label shown under the bar, for example "£10 off £50", and the ‘amount to go’ message with [amount], for example "Spend [amount] more to get £10 off".
  6. Optional: write a product teaser message. It shows under qualifying products until the reward is reached.
  7. For a second reward, do the same for checkpoint 3.
  8. Write the All checkpoints complete message and click Save.
Subscriptions don't count Only one-time purchases count towards spend rewards. To count subscriptions too, tick Include subscription items in checkpoint progress.

Run a free gift promotion

Once the cart has a product from the qualifying collection, the shopper can pick one free product from the gift collection.

  1. In Shopify admin › Products › Collections, make a collection of the gift products, if there isn't one.
  2. In Shopify admin › Discounts, create a Buy X get Y automatic discount: buy from the qualifying collection, get one product from the gift collection free. Set its dates.
  3. Go to Theme editor › Theme settings › Carts › Free gift chooser and tick Enable.
  4. Choose the Qualifying collection and the Gift products collection: the same as in the discount.
  5. Write the Panel title and the button texts.
  6. In Show under each gift's product name, choose the price it's worth or the number of servings.
  7. Write the Success banner message. Put [product] where the gift's name should go, for example "Your FREE [product] is in the bag!".
  8. Click Save.

Show free gifts on their own

  • Group free gift products into their own cart section: free items show under a Free Gifts heading in the slide-in cart.
  • Show discount name under product title in cart: shows the discount's name, for example the promotion title, under each discounted item.

Both are in Theme editor › Theme settings › Carts and change the cart for every product, not just the promotion.

Offer a gift at a spend amount

  1. Go to Theme editor › Theme settings › Add to cart Area › in cart threshold offer and tick Enable in cart offer.
  2. Set the Free gift threshold in pence. To show the gift when a certain product is in the cart instead, choose a Free gift product.
  3. Choose the Gift product and add up to two variants with their image, title and button text.
  4. Write the Offer Text and the Add Offer Text, shown once the shopper qualifies.
  5. To only show the offer once the shopper qualifies, untick Show teaser.
  6. Click Save.

Offer an add-on

  1. In Shopify admin › Products › [product] › Metafields, choose the add-on in Cross sell product.
  2. On the add-on product, write its short text in Cross sell description.
  3. Click Save. The add-on shows with an Add on + button when the first product is in the cart.

Before a promotion goes live

Good to know

  • Amounts are the same in every market. To use a different amount in one market, ask a developer.
  • Changes in Theme settings apply to the whole website as soon as you click Save.
  • To stop the cart opening when certain products are added, paste their variant IDs, separated by commas, in Theme settings › Add to cart Area › Suppress sidecart for variant IDs.
  • Free delivery and gift messages on product pages are in Promos & Products.
